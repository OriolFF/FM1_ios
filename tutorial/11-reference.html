The 32 algorithms

Yellow boxes are carriers (heard), blue boxes are modulators. A line from one operator down to another means “modulates”. A red arrow is feedback: on a single operator it means it modulates itself; in algorithms 4 and 6 the feedback loop runs from a higher operator back to a lower one.

carriermodulatorfeedback
About these drawings

The routing (who modulates whom, which operators are carriers, where feedback sits) matches the DX7 algorithm table that the FM-1 uses. The boxes are laid out by a simple program, so they do not copy the FM-1 screen’s exact positions.

Glossary

Algorithm
The wiring diagram that decides which operators modulate which, and which are heard. 32 choices.
AMD / PMD
Amplitude / Pitch Modulation Depth: how strongly the LFO wobbles volume / pitch.
Bank
A group of 32 voices; the FM-1 has A, B, C, D.
Break point
The key at which keyboard level scaling changes direction.
Carrier
An operator whose output you hear.
Coarse / Fine
The whole and decimal parts of an operator’s frequency ratio.
Detune
A very small pitch offset of one operator.
EG
Envelope generator: the 4-rate, 4-level contour of an operator or of the pitch.
Feedback
An operator modulating itself; makes sine → saw → noise.
Fixed frequency
An operator that ignores the key and plays one set pitch.
Gate
Length of each arpeggiator or sequencer note within its step.
Glide
A slide between notes (mono mode only).
LFO
Low frequency oscillator, for vibrato or tremolo.
Modulator
An operator that changes the pitch of another one and is not itself heard.
Operator
One sine oscillator with an envelope. The FM-1 has six.
Preset / Voice / Patch
One complete sound. The panel says “preset”; DX7 literature says “voice”.
Ratio
An operator’s frequency relative to the note played.
SysEx
System Exclusive: MIDI messages that carry data such as sound banks.
VMEM / VCED
The DX7’s 32-voice bank format / single-voice format.

Troubleshooting

ProblemTry
No soundRaise MASTER; check headphones/speaker; check operator OutLevels and that carriers are not muted (long-press mutes; changing voice un-mutes).
Keys do not play notesYou may be in EDIT mode (black keys are selectors) or in ARP/SEQ mode. Press HOME.
My edits disappearedYou changed voice before holding SAVE.
PRESETS does nothingSEQ is on. Press SEQ to leave, or change the pattern’s Voice.
“Disable SEQ to enter” / “Disable ARP to enter”Switch off the other one first.
No vibratoLFO: both PMD and Pitch Sens (page 2) must be above 0.
Glide does nothingGlide needs MONO and Glide Val above 0.
Mod wheel changes the filterThe mod wheel is on the Effect channel (default 2). Use another channel.
Bluetooth not foundLong-press HOME; the LED should blink. Connect from a MIDI app, not the system Bluetooth list (iPad).
Notes stuck or doubledCheck that only one MIDI source plays the same notes; USB, Bluetooth and TRS work at once.
Updater says “Failed to enter upgrade mode!”Keep the updater open, unplug and replug USB, continue (M-VAVE’s advice).

What we could not confirm

These details are not written down in the official manual, the MIDI guide, the release notes or the reviews we could read. Do not trust anyone (including this guide) who states them as fact without testing on a real unit.

  • The step-by-step firmware update procedure (button combination, if any).
  • The full list of ARP / SEQ Rate values and the default.
  • How ARP Octave interacts with each Mode.
  • What exactly Init Voice sets (the DX7 template is assumed).
  • Whether MONO/POLY and Glide are stored per voice or globally.
  • Factory effect and ENV settings for each of the 128 voices.
  • The effect algorithms; the mapping between the panel Filter Q (0–100) and the MIDI Q (0–10); the cut-off step table.
  • What aftertouch and mod wheel control exactly.
  • Whether a single-voice SysEx dump is auto-saved.
  • Details of Chain (looping), long-note and rest recording behaviour.
  • The Bluetooth name the unit advertises.
  • The PIT “no change” level; the display format of operator frequency.
  • Whether the OP mute mirror bug exists on a given unit.
