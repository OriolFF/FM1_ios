GLO page 1: MIDI & Pitch

Press GLO. These settings belong to the instrument, not to a voice. The screen title reads “Global” (or “Globe”).

KnobSettingRangeMeaning
K1MIDI ChALL, 1–16Which channel the FM-1 listens to for notes, pitch bend and program changes. ALL listens to every channel (omni). Called “Note Channel” before V13.
K2Effect Ch1–16The channel on which MIDI CC messages control the effects. Default 2. There is no ALL.
K3Bend Up1–24Maximum upward pitch bend, in semitones.
K4Bend Down1–24Maximum downward pitch bend, in semitones.
Mod wheel conflict

A MIDI CC1 (mod wheel) message sent on the effect channel (default 2) does not move the LFO: it changes the Filter Type. Send your keyboard’s mod wheel on another channel, or change Effect Ch.

GLO page 2: System

Turn SELECT clockwise.

KnobSettingRangeMeaning
K1Reset Patches—Clockwise opens a bank-restore dialog; see Voices.
K2Velocity1–127Fixed velocity for the on-board keys (V13+). Lower = softer for operators that respond to velocity.
K3Glide ModeFull Time / FingeredWhen glide applies (below).
K4Glide Val0–100Glide time, 0 to about 1 second.

Glide (portamento)

Glide makes the pitch slide from one note to the next instead of jumping. It only works in MONO (switch with EDIT → MONO).

  • Full Time: every new note glides from the previous one.
  • Fingered: it glides only when you play legato, i.e. press the new key while still holding the old one.
  • Glide Val: 0 = no glide, 100 = about one second.

MIDI

The FM-1 has three MIDI inputs that all work at the same time: USB (appears as a standard USB MIDI device), Bluetooth LE MIDI, and the 3.5 mm TRS MIDI IN (31,250 baud). Notes you play, the arpeggiator and the sequencer are sent out over USB and Bluetooth, so the FM-1 can also be a MIDI keyboard.

What it understands

MessageEffect

Aftertouch and mod wheel

The official MIDI guide lists “Modulation” and “Channel aftertouch” but does not say which parameter they control. In a DX7-style engine they drive the LFO’s pitch and amplitude depth; that is not confirmed for the FM-1.

Bluetooth

  1. Long-press HOME to switch Bluetooth on. Do the same to switch it off.

  2. Read the HOME LED: blinking = on, waiting for a connection; solid = connected; off = Bluetooth is off.

  3. On the other device, connect to the FM-1 as a Bluetooth MIDI device from inside a MIDI app (on iPad: the app’s Bluetooth MIDI menu; on Mac: Audio MIDI Setup → Bluetooth).

The name it advertises

Not documented. Look for the model name in your app’s Bluetooth MIDI list.

Effect channel CC chart (V15)

On the Effect Ch channel, CCs 0–23 control the effects. Values above the maximum are clamped.

EffectOn/OffParam 1Param 2Param 3
FilterCC 0CC 1 Type (0 LP, 1 BP, 2 HP)CC 2 Cutoff 0–107CC 3 Q 0–10
ReverbCC 4CC 5 Type (0 Room, 1 Hall, 2 Plate)CC 6 Decay 0–100CC 7 Mix 0–100
DelayCC 8CC 9 DecayCC 10 RateCC 11 Mix

ChorusCC 16CC 17 FreqCC 18 DepthCC 19 Mix

On/Off: 0 = off, 1 or higher = on. Delay, Distortion, Chorus and Phaser values are 0–100.

Careful: CC 7 is Reverb Mix here, not volume

On the effect channel, CC 7 does not control volume.

Firmware update

M-VAVE publishes firmware on its download page, and updates are installed from a computer with M-VAVE’s updater (M-UPGRADE, for Windows and Mac). What the official pages tell us:

  • The page says that if the upgrade reports “Failed to enter upgrade mode!”, keep the upgrade software open, unplug and re-plug the USB cable, then continue.
  • Every firmware has release notes (V09, V13, V15 and V16 exist). V13’s notes say it is delivered as a “major update”, which updates the whole firmware.
  • V15 is the version this guide covers. V16 (October 2026) is a bigger change: multitimbral, a new FX system, a SAVE menu, a new MIDI map.
The actual steps are not documented

We could not find an official step-by-step for the FM-1 (for example, whether a button must be held at power-up) in the manual or on M-VAVE’s pages. Follow the instructions that come with the updater download. Back up any voices you care about first; stock V15 cannot export them.

Going to V16 changes this guide

The panel basics stay the same, but the effects, SAVE, sequencer, GLO and MIDI pages differ in V16. This guide is about V15.

Battery and speaker

  • The battery icon is at the top right of the screen. A full-screen LOW BATTERY warning appears when it is critically low and stays until you charge (USB-C) or power off.
  • The built-in speaker is for monitoring; plugging headphones into the audio jack mutes it.
